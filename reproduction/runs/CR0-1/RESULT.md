# Gaussian minimum purification — independent clean-room run

## Status

**Outcome: PARTIAL.** I did not obtain a universal proof or a counterexample for either statistics. In particular, I do **not** promote the successful small numerical searches to a proof.

For the full finite-mode target in `TASK.md`:

| Domain | Value: arbitrary finite auxiliaries vs. matched `(n_A,n_B)` | Attainment by matched sizes |
|---|---|---|
| Bosons | **unresolved** | **unresolved** |
| Fermions | **unresolved** | **unresolved** |

What is proved below is: (i) an exact reduction of the missing step to a Gaussian Stinespring/output-size compression statement; (ii) universal bounds; (iii) the full target for a nontrivial exact family containing all pure states and all product states; and (iv) existence of a minimum for the **fixed matched-size fermionic subproblem** (which is weaker than attainment of the arbitrary-size infimum).

## 1. Restatement and conventions used

Let `N=n_A+n_B`. The question is whether the infimum of `S(AA')` over **all finite** pure Gaussian extensions of a fixed finite-mode Gaussian `rho_AB`, with arbitrary complete-mode auxiliary sizes `(m_A,m_B)`, is always achieved with exactly `m_A=n_A` and `m_B=n_B`.

I use the covariance and entropy normalizations in `CONVENTIONS.md`. For fermions I fix the complete-mode ordering

`A modes, B modes, A' modes, B' modes`,

with the two Majoranas of each complex mode adjacent. Reduction to a complete-mode subsystem means restriction to the CAR subalgebra generated by those modes; computationally one may first make the parity-correct fermionic permutation that makes the retained complete modes adjacent and then take the ordinary reduced density operator. For a parity-invariant Gaussian state this has the covariance obtained by the corresponding principal restriction, and its entropy is the sum of the one-mode functions `f(lambda)` from `CONVENTIONS.md`.

For bosons, first moments can be removed from the optimization: the given displacement splits as local displacements on `A` and `B`; applying or undoing these local Gaussian unitaries gives a bijection of Gaussian purifications and does not change `S(AA')`. Thus the value and the size question depend only on the covariance. The original finite first moments can be restored after constructing a purification.

## 2. Matched-size purifications always exist

This is an existence statement only; it is not the minimum-size conjecture.

### Bosons

Put the physical covariance in Williamson form

`V_AB = S [ direct_sum_j (nu_j I_2) ] S^T`, `nu_j >= 1`.

For every mixed normal mode `nu_j>1`, introduce one auxiliary mode and use the four-dimensional pure block

`V_j^pur = [[nu_j I_2, sqrt(nu_j^2-1) Z], [sqrt(nu_j^2-1) Z, nu_j I_2]]`,

where `Z=diag(1,-1)`. Direct multiplication verifies the pure-covariance relation with the two-mode symplectic form. A normal mode with `nu_j=1` is already pure and requires no active purifier. Apply `S` on the physical modes. If `r` is the number of `nu_j>1`, this gives a Gaussian purification with `r<=N` active auxiliary modes. Add `N-r` independent vacuum modes and label the resulting `N` auxiliaries as `n_A` modes of `A'` and `n_B` modes of `B'`. Hence `P_{n_A,n_B}(rho_AB)` is nonempty.

### Fermions

Put

`Gamma_AB = O [ direct_sum_j (lambda_j J) ] O^T`, `0<=lambda_j<=1`,

with `J=[[0,1],[-1,0]]`. For every `lambda_j<1`, set `s_j=sqrt(1-lambda_j^2)` and use

`Gamma_j^pur = [[lambda_j J, s_j K],[-s_j K, lambda_j J]]`,

where `K=[[0,1],[1,0]]`. Since `J^2=-I`, `K^2=I`, and `JK+KJ=0`, this block squares to `-I`, so it is pure. Modes with `lambda_j=1` need no active purifier. Applying `O` on the physical modes and filling to `N` auxiliary modes with independent pure fermionic modes proves that the matched set is nonempty, including boundary and repeated parameters.

This explicit construction agrees with the normal/purified standard forms in WJEH2021, Section 2.6, PDF pp. 17–18, eqs. (79) and (83), but the block checks above are the argument used here.

## 3. Two universal bounds for the arbitrary-size Gaussian problem

For every allowed Gaussian state,

`(1/2) I(A:B) <= E_P^G(rho_AB) <= min{S(A),S(B)}`.

The lower bound does not use Gaussianity. For any pure extension `Psi_AA'BB'`,

`I(A:B) <= I(AA':BB') = 2 S(AA')`,

where the inequality is data processing under the local traces `AA' -> A` and `BB' -> B`. Taking the infimum gives the lower bound.

For the upper bound, take the finite canonical Gaussian purification using `r<=N` active purifier modes. Assign **all** purifier modes to `B'` and take `A'` empty; then the global state is pure and the objective is `S(A)`. Assigning all purifier modes to `A'` instead gives `S(B)`. This uses the permission `m_A,m_B in N_0` in the packet.

These upper bounds are for the arbitrary-size problem. They do not by themselves show that the matched-size subproblem achieves the same values.

## 4. Exact reduction to the missing compression lemma

Let `R` be a minimal active Gaussian purifier of `rho_AB`, with `r` modes, and choose a fixed pure Gaussian `|Omega>_{ABR}`. Put

`sigma_AR = Tr_B |Omega><Omega|`.

A supplied-source premise used here is the Gaussian purification uniqueness/orbit statement: Gaussian purifications of a fixed Gaussian marginal are related by a Gaussian transformation acting on the purifying system (WJEH2021, PDF p. 15, eqs. (70)–(71)); Section 2.6, pp. 17–18, also explicitly allows additional uncorrelated auxiliary modes. Thus an arbitrary finite Gaussian purification can be represented by adjoining finitely many pure Gaussian modes `F`, applying a Gaussian unitary/isometry on `R F`, and declaring its output modes to be `A' B'`.

Equivalently, let `Phi:R -> A'` range over finite-mode Gaussian channels admitting a **pure Gaussian Stinespring dilation**, with complementary output `B'`. Then exactly

`E_P^G(rho_AB) = inf_Phi S( (id_A tensor Phi)(sigma_AR) )`.

The matched-size conjecture is therefore equivalent, at the value level, to the following state-dependent compression assertion:

> **Gaussian Stinespring compression residual.** For the `sigma_AR` arising from every finite Gaussian `rho_AB`, every finite pure Gaussian Stinespring isometry `R -> A' B'` can be replaced (for purposes of this entropy objective) by one with exactly `n_A` output modes in `A'_0` and exactly `n_B` complementary output modes in `B'_0`, with no larger value of `S(A A'_0)`.

A matched dilation starts with `r` active purifier modes plus `N-r` pure modes, applies a Gaussian transformation on those `N` modes, and splits the outputs as `(n_A,n_B)`.

I did not prove this compression assertion. It pinpoints the part that is not established merely by exhibiting one matched-size purification.

For bosons there is a second logically separate issue: even if equality of the two infima follows from such a compression theorem, the matched symplectic parameter space is noncompact, so one still needs an attainment/coercivity argument to replace the matched infimum by a minimum. I did not complete that argument.

For fermions, this second issue does not occur at fixed matched size; see Section 6.

## 5. An exact family for which the full target holds

Suppose that, after local Gaussian unitaries on `A` and `B`, the state factors as

`rho_AB = |psi><psi|_{A_c B_c} tensor sigma_{A_l} tensor tau_{B_l}`,

where `|psi>` is a pure Gaussian state carrying all cross-party correlations and `sigma`, `tau` are arbitrary local Gaussian states. The mode decompositions `A=A_c+A_l` and `B=B_c+B_l` may include pure factors and boundary normal parameters.

Then, for both bosons and fermions,

`E_P^G(rho_AB) = S(psi_{A_c})`,

and this value is attained with exactly `m_A=n_A`, `m_B=n_B`.

**Proof.** Mutual information is additive on tensor products, the local factors contribute zero mutual information, and the pure correlated factor contributes

`I(A:B)=2 S(psi_{A_c})`.

The universal lower bound therefore gives `S(AA') >= S(psi_{A_c})` for every purification.

For the matching upper construction, purify `sigma_{A_l}` using auxiliary modes placed entirely in `A'`, purify `tau_{B_l}` using auxiliary modes placed entirely in `B'`, and fill all unused matched auxiliary slots by independent pure Gaussian modes. The `A_l A'_used` factor is pure and entirely on the left side of the objective cut; the analogous `B_l B'_used` factor is entirely on the right. The only entanglement across `AA' | BB'` is therefore the original pure factor `|psi>_{A_cB_c}`, giving exactly `S(psi_{A_c})`. Local Gaussian unitaries used to reach the displayed factorization can be restored without changing the entropy.

This family contains, in particular:

- every pure bipartite Gaussian state (take the whole state as the pure correlated factor);
- every product Gaussian state (take the correlated factor to be trivial), for which the value is `0`;
- the empty-side cases;
- arbitrary local mixed or pure Gaussian factors appended to a pure correlated core.

Thus the packet's boundary cases do not by themselves provide a counterexample.

## 6. Fermionic fixed-matched-size attainment and parity

This section proves a universal fact about the **right-hand matched-size optimization**, not the equality with the arbitrary-size infimum.

Fix `m_A=n_A`, `m_B=n_B`, so there are `N` auxiliary fermionic modes. Choose any reference matched Gaussian purification. By the Gaussian purification orbit statement cited above, all matched Gaussian purifications in a fixed parity component are obtained by auxiliary orthogonal transformations. The relevant group component is compact (`SO(2N)`); allowing both total parities gives the union inside compact `O(2N)`. The reduced covariance depends continuously on that transformation, and the entropy function `f(lambda)` is continuous also at `lambda=0,1`. Therefore the matched-size fermionic subproblem has a genuine minimum for every finite input state, including degeneracies and pure factors.

The two total-parity components have the same set of objective values when `N>0`: multiplying the auxiliary covariance coordinates by any determinant-`-1` orthogonal transformation supported wholly in `A'` (or, if needed, wholly in `B'`) crosses the two components while leaving the physical `AB` block fixed; on the retained covariance it is an orthogonal congruence and hence leaves the entropy spectrum unchanged. This is a covariance-level statement and does not identify determinant-`-1` maps with parity-preserving quadratic evolutions. When `N=0` the problem is trivial.

WJEH2021, PDF p. 3, explicitly distinguishes the full fermionic pure-Gaussian manifold `O(2N)/U(N)` from the fixed-parity restriction using `SO(2N)`, which is the source premise used for this parity-component description.

What remains unresolved is whether the arbitrary finite-size infimum is equal to this attained matched minimum.

## 7. Audit of the supplied paper's Conjecture 2 passage

WJEH2021 states the minimum purification conjecture on PDF p. 43, Section 6.1. The same paper's PDF p. 46 says that one can restrict the auxiliary systems to the sizes of `A` and `B`, then constructs a matched-size purification in eqs. (191)–(192).

In the supplied text I did **not** find an argument there that compares an arbitrary larger purification with a matched one and proves that the entropy cannot decrease. The construction establishes that a matched-size purification exists and supplies a useful starting point/upper candidate; by itself it does not establish the inequality needed to exclude larger Stinespring dilations. I therefore did not import the p. 46 sentence as a theorem resolving the present task.

The paper's Table 6 on PDF p. 44 is numerical evidence for selected models and fixed finite sizes. I treated it only as evidence, not as a premise.

## 8. Numerical diagnostics performed in this run

All numerical experiments were written during this run in `RUN_OUTPUT/`; none is used as a universal proof.

- `fermion_extra_scan_fast.py`: 12 random `n_A=n_B=1` fermionic states. Local optimization over matched `1+1` auxiliaries and over `1+2` / `2+1` auxiliaries found no convincing strict improvement from the extra mode; values agreed to roughly `10^-14`–`10^-15` in the reported runs.
- `fermion_1x2_extra_scan.py`: 4 random `n_A=1,n_B=2` states; matched `1+2` and one-extra-mode `1+3` / `2+2` searches likewise produced no value below the matched result beyond roundoff-scale differences.
- `fermion_unbalanced_scan.py`: 40 random/boundary two-mode states compared a locally optimized matched value with the exact arbitrary-size candidates `S(A)` and `S(B)` obtained by putting all active purifier modes on the opposite side. No tested matched result exceeded `min(S(A),S(B))`.
- `boson_search.py`: four structured two-mode bosonic states compared matched `1+1` with one-extra-mode searches. No run found a value below the best matched value. Importantly, in one case the local optimizer for an enlarged `2+1` parameterization returned a value **above** the matched value even though the enlarged set contains the matched construction with an added pure local mode. This is direct evidence that the optimizer can get stuck and is why none of these numerical outcomes is treated as a global comparison.
- `fermion_search.py`: an earlier, heavier scan exceeded the command time limit after producing partial data; it is retained only as a failed diagnostic attempt.

These experiments are consistent with the conjecture in the sampled cases but do not resolve the universal statement.

## 9. Remaining mathematical gaps

A complete positive solution would still need, for both statistics, the Gaussian Stinespring compression residual in Section 4, including arbitrary finite output sizes, zero-sized sides, pure normal modes, repeated parameters, and all boundary values.

For bosons it would additionally need a proof that the matched-size infimum is attained by a normal finite-covariance Gaussian purification rather than only approached along an unbounded symplectic sequence. Numerical success cannot replace this.

A negative solution would instead need an explicit finite Gaussian `rho_AB` and a verifiable larger/unbalanced Gaussian purification with entropy strictly below the **global** matched-size infimum, or a rigorous bosonic nonattainment sequence. I did not obtain either.
